Safety check

Review these with the client before proceeding.

⛔ Cannot proceed if client has:
  • Active cancer or malignant tumors
  • Pregnancy or actively trying to conceive
  • Pacemaker or implanted electronic device (ICD, neurostimulator, cochlear implant, insulin pump)
  • Active bleeding disorder or taking blood thinners
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorder
  • Recent heart attack or stroke (within 6 months)
  • Organ transplant / immunosuppressant medications
  • Fever or active systemic infection
⚠️ Review with staff before proceeding:
  • Intra-articular injection (cortisone, PRP) within last 6 months
  • Recent surgery within 8 weeks in the area to be worked on
  • Metal implants (plates, screws, rods) near treatment area
  • Thrombocytopenia (low platelet count)
  • Currently taking opioid medications
  • Undiagnosed pain — confirm diagnosis before selecting protocol
